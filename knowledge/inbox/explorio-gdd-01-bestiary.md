# Bestiary

## Monster Classification

### Based on physical body

- Mechanical (Automatons, Clockworks)
- Organic (Animals, Corpselike undeads, Most Humanoids)
- Elemental (self explanatory)
- Incorporeal (Ghosts, Illusions, Energy creations)

### Based on intelligence

- Elders (Beyond humanoid comprehension)
- Humanoids (Between scholars and tribalistic creatures)
- Beasts (Follows instincts and some logic)
- Critters (Barely sentient)

### Based on biological division

- Reptile (Dinosaurs Dragons and other magical reptiles)
- Arthropod (Scorpions, Crabs and Insects)
- Amphibious (Aquatic races, anything that needs water to live)
- Avian (Feathered races, birds)

### Based on relation to life

- Immortal (removed from lifecycle)
- Undead (dead temporarily returned to life)

### Based on plane of existence

- Demonic (native to cursed plane of existence)
- Ethereal (native to blessed plane of existence)
- Ancient (been alive since the origin of the world)

### Based on related cultures

- Dwarven (made by dwarves)
- Pirate (belongs to pirates)
- Atlantic (made by aquatic races)
- [Of Dunes] (made by desert races)
- Elven (made by forest elfs)
- Artificial (mortal made life created in laboratory)

## Elemental Lifeforms

![](explorio-gdd-01-bestiary-images/image020.png)![](explorio-gdd-01-bestiary-images/image021.png)![](explorio-gdd-01-bestiary-images/image022.png)![](explorio-gdd-01-bestiary-images/image023.png)![](explorio-gdd-01-bestiary-images/image024.png)![](explorio-gdd-01-bestiary-images/image025.png)

### Description

Creatures made of pure elemental energies.

They are aligned with one (sometimes more) of elements:

- Water
- Earth
- Fire
- Light
- Dark
- Life
- Death
- Air
- Fell
- Lightning

### Behavior

- Hostility
  - Elemental creatures from opposite sides of a chart are often hostile one to another.
- Resistances
  - Elemental creatures are immune to their own element
- Attacks
  - All elementals can make basic attacks with their aligned element. Slimes perform them on touch and short range while creatures can do them on a long range as well.
- Movement
  - It also depends on the species. Slimes have small jumps and long jumps with a wind up. Spawns use moveset of their animal form. Elementals move steadily at a constant speed.
- Where can be found
  - All elemental lifeforms prefer to stay in an environment that matches their alignment. Slimes can be found near mineral deposits, because they like to digest them.
  - Swallowed items are visible from outside.

		![](explorio-gdd-01-bestiary-images/image026.png)![](explorio-gdd-01-bestiary-images/image027.png)

- Mechanics
  - They can have a **[ Key ]** inside them
  - When slimes splits **[ Key ]** have a chance to drop on the ground but other slimes can swallow him again
  - If **[ Key ]** doesn’t drop on the ground it is passed to lower size slime
- Loot
  - Manifestations and Spawns drop Elemental Runes on death. Slimes drops swallowed items.
  - Slime splatters can be picked up with a bottle.

## Golems

### Description

Golems magically animated objects. Their power is dependent on the materials they are made of. Many golems have similar powers to elemental creatures. All golems are highly resistant to magic. Golems often look similar to their creators 

- Dwarven golems have beards carved in its body.   
![](explorio-gdd-01-bestiary-images/image079.png)![](explorio-gdd-01-bestiary-images/image080.png)
- Cultists paint their rituals and handprints on golems bodies and shape their head hoodlike.
- ~~Giants give horned heads to theirs.~~
- ~~Orcs put up spears and totems on theirs~~  
![](explorio-gdd-01-bestiary-images/image081.png)
- Nagas make golems from giant stone segments connected to the shape of a snake  
![](explorio-gdd-01-bestiary-images/image082.png)

Creators also can shape golem hands to be ax-shaped or hammer-shaped if those or any other weapon have significant value in their culture.  
![](explorio-gdd-01-bestiary-images/image083.png)

### Behavior

- Slam attack if enemy nearby
  - Specialized golems (holding weapons or having weapons for hands have attack with this weapon instead)
- Charge if enemy in a distance
  - Golems that can use spells or throw a weapon can choose to do it instead
- Enrage
  - Gain Haste for a short time
- Resistances
  - Golems are immune to most status effects unless material they are made of is very weak to that specific effect.

Golems drop pieces for materials they are made from when they die. If they were holding weapons those can drop too and specialized hands can drop as a heavy two handed variant of those weapons.

# Enemy AI

## Lure and deterrence

### Things enemies can be deterred by:

- Smell of undead/poison
- Lightsource (fire, magic, lava)
- Loud sound (bell)
- Sight of pointy weapons
- Sight of killer of their friends (pack of critters will run away if you kill enough of them)
- Large size of creature (predator, imposing presence)
- Fear spell strongly deterrence its target from the spellcaster

### Things enemies can be lured by:

- Smell of things they like (booze, brew, honey, blood, cheese)
- Light sound
- Sight of prey (predator monsters only)
- Sight of item on the ground (precious, useful, unique)
- Sight of water in desert area (splatters or in bottle)
- Charm spell passivies and strongly lures its target to the  spellcaster.

### Examples

- Player can use spears or torches to scare away small critters so that those monsters pose no danger.
- Player throws a deterrent into a room through the bars to force the enemy to pull the lever so that it can escape, opening all doors to the room.  
![](explorio-gdd-01-bestiary-images/image263.png)
- Player can throw cheese bar next to the sleeping rat so that it will wake up and move from pressure plate it has been sleeping on
- Player can leave shiny item on the floor to try to catch gremlin
- Player can splash booze on the floor to lure one orc out of it’s guard
- Player can splash brew on the enemies to lure wolves into attacking them
