# Noita-style staff enhancement ideas

Navigation: [[wiki/maps/explorio-knowledge-map|Knowledge map]]

GDD 0x's *Noita > Interesting effects* table pairs Noita screenshots with
captions. The images are not read, so the underlying Noita item is often
unknown; only the caption text is recorded. "Sigil" appears as a staff
enhancement slot.

## Environment-changing effects

Highest value for puzzles and gating (synthesis on the right).

| Caption (shortened) | Puzzle use |
|---|---|
| Rain, possibly a scroll spell: strong against some elementals; may also give caster regeneration or magic resistance; combos with a grimoire school that works with Wet; **creates water puddles and extinguishes open fire** | key for Wet-based reactions and for Burning bridges / flammable barricades |
| Oil-like status that **removes floor traction**; part of a **parkour challenge on a narrow passage between chasms** | Parkour archetype hazard; matches Slippery in GDD 04 |
| Spells leave **gas trails** of their element (sparks and smoke for fire, steam for water) | spreads element to the environment |
| Scrolls of item, gold and life detection, area mapping, fog-of-war removal | CLUE keys; counters to Lost / Blinded |
| Mana-infused blood splatters regenerate mana | links to the Mana infused status |
| Blessed spell: light blast at a location, exploding after a delay | radiant source (Blessed vs Undead/Cursed) |
| Shield effect that returns projectiles | – |

## Modifier ideas

| Caption (shortened) | Kind |
|---|---|
| Reduces damage but adds area of effect | enhancement or staff alt-attack |
| Heavy shot as an earth sigil; opposite-element sigil gives a second effect; speed is not a useful stat here | sigil |
| Spells that don't alarm enemies like projectiles do – for a trickster/rogue | stealth |
| Spread, possibly with a chance to use up a charge | modifier |
| Self-cast for offensive spells, or a unique staff mechanic | modifier |
| Spell copies itself on impact with much less damage (easy, scalable) | modifier |
| Cursed or unique bat staff, maybe multishot; strong with a gas trail | staff |
| Second damage instance with a piercing tag | upgrade |
| Extra status on the target, one per element or propagating any damage type | modifier |
| Status that triggers an element-based effect when the enemy dies | modifier |
| Mana-related effect: small impact since casting is limited by player mana, not staff charge | note |
| Changes target resistance to the spell's element for a while | debuff |
| Casts twice, big charge-cost reduction | modifier |
| Curse like Unexplored's Staff of Fiery Doom | cursed staff |
| Cursed staff paying health instead of mana, big charge-cost reduction | cursed staff |
| Good with an added slow on the enemy | modifier |
| Cursed wand idea; nature spell idea; air spell idea; vampirism idea | placeholders |

| Armour blessing: small passive, stronger with no (specific) statuses active | armour |

## Notes

- One caption says casting is "limited by player mana, not the amount of spell
  power left in a staff", while another describes a cursed staff that takes
  health "when applying spells to the wand". Together with GDD 04 (staves
  charged with mana) this suggests a mana pool that charges staves, but the
  exact model is not settled in either doc.
