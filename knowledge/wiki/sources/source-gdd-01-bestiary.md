## Summary

The bestiary is a design brainstorm of Explorio's creatures. It opens with six
orthogonal classification axes (body, intelligence, biological division,
relation to life, plane of existence, culture), then lists about twenty monster
families: critters, elemental lifeforms (slimes, spawns, manifestations, genies),
golems, land-walking, flying and amphibious humanoids (orcs with tribes and
classes, goblins, rat men, kobolds, trolls, nagas…), hybrids, cursed humanoids
(werebeasts, vampires), mimics, arthropods (including "mirage arthropods" that
disguise as levers, pressure plates and hourglasses), magical reptiles, void
monstrosities, undead, sylvanids, dwarven automatons, pirates and demons. Many
entries tie a monster to a location (environment) or to an encounter theme, and
many give it an interaction beyond combat: carrying or swallowing a key, powering
doors, digging paths, stealing items, alarming others. It closes with an "Enemy
AI" chapter on lures and deterrents with puzzle examples; its "Enemy tactics"
section is empty.
