## Summary

A design notebook on bosses, difficulty and plot. It defines a boss by *real
threat* and *unique mechanics*, then lists boss, mini-boss, gauntlet, elite and
mounted-boss ideas; the Lorekeeper (desert lich) is worked out in detail as a
room with gates, timed buttons, phylacteries and a collapse timer. The
difficulty part sketches an enemy-composition pipeline, a challenge-rating
curve per layer (headings only), biome-bound power collectibles, an Ascension
("frenzy") meter, and a long pros/cons study of backtracking with mitigations
(teleportation, unique workstations, lost-and-found, cyclic generation). The
plot part proposes a three-act run with main bosses A, B and C and the Five
Room Dungeon as the per-biome structure, plus ~20 plot hooks. For the
generator, the important content is bosses as locks/keys, the per-biome room
template and the statement that cyclic generation removes backtracking.
