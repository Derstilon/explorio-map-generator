## Summary

An item design notebook. It opens with gear-logic questions (why wizards
avoid armour, stealth vs plate, critical strikes, armour penetration), then
lists rings, cloaks, armour stats (incl. toolbar slots, visibility, noise),
belts/bags, weapons (range, upgrades, quality, types, damage types),
grimoires and magic foci. Consumables cover rations (several act as lures or
clues), a potion schema and ~21 potion types, and scrolls (identification,
casting time, burning, offensive/utility/summoning). "Other" lists tools
(crowbar, lockpicks, rope, torch…), containers, and empty *Keys*, *Orbs* and
*Figures* stubs. It ends with runic enchantments, three curse sets (seven
sins, four horsemen, plagues of Egypt) and unique items such as the *Friendly
mimic key*. For the generator, the value is the list of items that can serve as
keys and the hints on which are reusable, consumable, breakable or on
cooldown.
