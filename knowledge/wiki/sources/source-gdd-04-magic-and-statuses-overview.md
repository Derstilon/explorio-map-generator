## Summary

An early, partly empty design doc for magic and status effects. It states why
spellcasting exists (depth, recognisable enemy abilities the player can take
over, a limited "get out of jail" card), lists five Brogue-inspired spells,
names six base spell schools and three quasi elements (all sections empty),
proposes health-threshold indicators, six damage-over-time mechanic types, and a
long list of about 60 statuses. Several statuses are defined by their
interaction with elements (Wet with Electric/Fire/Cold, Burning with Wet and
plants) or with terrain (Levitation, Flying, Slippery, Hidden, Amphibious),
which makes them candidate keys and locks for the story graph. Some entries are
headings only; two lines are in Polish.

## Section outline

- The point of spellcasting
- Spells
  - Base Spellschools: Pyromancy, Water, Power, Air, Earth, Wild
  - Quasi Elements: Wood, Metal, Magma
- Status Effects
  - Visual indicators: Constitution indicators, Status indicators
  - Damage over time: Visualization, Types (Percentage, Decreasing, Increasing,
    Constant, Evenly from pool, Ticking bomb)
  - Statuses
