# Explorio | 06 | World Generation

Navigation: [[wiki/maps/explorio-knowledge-map|Knowledge map]]

- Google Doc id: `1Lw6ksUrsxd6SWVOk31BEr-Eyu-SUr8easvKtGFK6G6M`
- Internal title: *World Atlas – An In-Depth Exploration of Explorio's Themes and Traps*
- Language: English (one Polish line, translated)

## Summary

The game-design document for the world and its generation. It sets up the
cosmology (six elements with three alignments, quasi-elements, crystal dragons),
a rune language meant as a puzzle-clue system, pantheons of action and virtue
gods, and theme overlays (race, magic, boss) that redress biomes. Its dungeon
chapter lists walkable-area and room-purpose generation methods (mostly empty
headings), eight room types with shape primitives, 41 example biomes with
layout hints and a to-do list of biome attributes, and a large catalogue of
dungeon contents: containers, trap / puzzle / encounter intents, environmental
hazards, and named puzzle patterns such as Key burden and Compound puzzles. A
short narrative section frames the game as a campaign of altered seeds. Game
Setting, Locations, Interactables and most generation-method subsections are
empty; several diagrams are images only and were not ingested.

## Section outline

- World Building – Cosmology; Languages; Cosmology Diagram Ideas (images);
  Pantheons; Themes (Table of Themes, Race themes, Magic themes, Boss Themes:
  Draconic, Orcish, Trollish, Lich, Mimic, Vampiric, Werewolf)
- Game Setting – World before / after creation of the Dungeon (headings only)
- Narrative – Sections: Macguffins, Betrayal, Finale
- Dungeon Generation – Generation strategies (Walkable area generation methods;
  Room purpose classification methods); Generation system graphs (Brogue
  Architect, Dungeon Architect, Room types); Locations (empty); Biomes (To do,
  Examples); Dungeon contents (Chests and Containers, Interactables (empty),
  Traps, Puzzles, Monster Encounters, Environmental Hazards and ~25 named
  puzzle/trap patterns); Materials (Liquids, Gasses, Tiles)
