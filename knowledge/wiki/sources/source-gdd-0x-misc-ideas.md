## Summary

A grab-bag doc. Its useful parts are a seed-save design (seeds with version and
date, save folders per character, three slots like Unexplored), open art-style
choices (2D/2.5D/3D, vector/pixel/raster, animation-driven hitboxes), and a list
of reference games with what to take from each. For the generator the key lines
are that **Brogue** sets the target level-generation quality and **Unexplored**
is the game to beat. Unexplored 2 contributes UI and combat features; Noita
contributes ~30 captioned staff/wand enhancement ideas, a few of which change
the environment (rain puddles, oil, gas trails, detection scrolls). Music and
naming-convention sections are empty.

## Section outline

- Game Menu Features: Seed Save, Naming convention
- Music / Soundtrack
- Graphic / Art Style: Perspective, Item Graphics, Character Animations, Spell
  Visual Effects
- Other similar games: Cogmind, Unexplored, Sipho, Unexplored 2 (Investigate
  Feature, Target information, Combat, Fortune Tests), Brogue, Rogue,
  Warcraft/Warhammer/DnD/Witcher 3, Genshin/Elden Ring, Graveyard Keeper, Battle
  Brothers, Caves of Qud, Noita (Interesting effects), Heroes of Might and
  Magic 3, Outward
